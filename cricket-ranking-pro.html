ℹ️ How Ratings are Calculated

Unified 0 - 100 Rating Scale

Each player earns a composite rating derived from batting, bowling, fielding, and sportsmanship. Unplayed formats are never penalized.

🏏 Batting Score (0 - 45 pts): Runs, Batting Average (benchmarked vs format standards), and Strike Rate.
🎯 Bowling Score (0 - 45 pts): Wickets, Bowling Economy, and Bowling Average.
🧤 Fielding Bonus (0 - 5 pts): Catches, Run-Outs, and Stumpings.
🤝 Fair Play Score (0 - 5 pts): Player conduct and umpire respect rating.

In ALL-FORMAT mode, ratings are dynamically averaged across active formats without zero-penalties.
